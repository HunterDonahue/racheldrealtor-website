If you've spent any time house-hunting in Fort Worth, you've probably had 76109 come up more than once. It's a big zip code with a lot packed into it — TCU sits right in the middle, and the neighborhoods that ring it (Mistletoe Heights, Berkeley Place, Bluebonnet Hills, Park Hill, Colonial Hills) each have a slightly different personality even though they're a five-minute drive from each other.

## Why buyers keep asking about it

Three things come up in almost every conversation I have about this area:

**Walkability that doesn't feel like an afterthought.** A lot of Fort Worth is built around the car, understandably. 76109 is one of the few places where you can genuinely walk to a coffee shop, a park, and a restaurant without treating it like an event. That matters more to buyers than it used to.

**Tree cover and lot size, close in.** Mistletoe Heights and Berkeley Place both have mature trees and lots that are bigger than what you'll find in newer close-in development. If you want character and canopy without a 30-minute commute, this is where people end up looking.

**A real range of price points.** This isn't a one-price-band zip code. You'll find everything from smaller bungalows that need work to fully renovated homes near the top of the Fort Worth market, often on the same street. That range is part of why it stays competitive — there's an entry point for more buyers than people assume.

## What to actually watch for

A few things I tell every buyer looking in this area, regardless of budget:

- **Foundation and plumbing age matter more than curb appeal.** A lot of this housing stock is 60-90 years old. A home that looks perfectly renovated on the surface can still be sitting on original cast iron or an aging foundation. This is exactly the kind of thing to get a real inspection on, not skip because the kitchen looks new.
- **Parking and lot access vary block to block.** Some of these streets were platted before anyone thought about a two-car household. Worth a real look before you fall in love with a floor plan.
- **Renovation permits are worth pulling.** If a home's been updated, ask whether the work was permitted. It affects both safety and resale down the line.
